How small-sample rankings work (updated methodology): handicapped golf regresses to the mean, so pace gets blended with the field average, weighted by rounds played — more rounds = trust their own number more. As of this update the shrink is tiered by sample size: 4+ rounds barely adjusted, 3 rounds pulled harder toward the field average, and 2 rounds pulled hardest of all. Sensitivity check: even at extreme shrink (94% toward the field average), a hot 2-round pace can still out-rank players with real bad rounds already on their card — regression alone can't fix that, so there's also a hard rule: no player with only 2 rounds played can rank inside the top 60, regardless of their pace. They're still tracked and still have real Floor/Ceiling/Projection numbers, just held below the group until a 3rd round exists to trust. 3-round players sitting near the cutoff (ranks ~35-65) get flagged in their comment as still-unproven.
